# Kinds: the stat sheet

Every kind a story holds (a world-book entry with a body, or a kind drawn by the seed) has a sheet with every field filled (`src/rules/kind-sheet.ts`). The player's rule: an empty field is worse than a small one, and a value written here is better than one a model decides. Each field below gives where its value comes from: **data** (a record the engine holds), **rolled** (drawn from data), **computed** (worked out by the engine), or **default** (a human's value, written down, where nothing is known). The shape of the sheet is what roguelikes give a creature (NetHack: speed, resistances, attacks, size; Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead: hit points, speed with a human at 100, sight, flags such as SEES, HEARS and SMELLS, regeneration, reproduction; Dwarf Fortress: body, tokens, attribute ranges); the values come from the Dwarf Fortress creature files inside the engine (`rules/df/raw`, public domain), the engine's own models, and the papers named.

| Group | Field | Value | From | Read by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body | what it lacks | breath, warmth of its own, sleep, our food, drink, pain, fear: Dwarf Fortress tokens (NOBREATHE in 18 of the raws' 767 creatures, NO_SLEEP 64, NO_EAT 15, NO_DRINK 97, NOPAIN 21, NOFEAR 20; warmth kept by 289) and NO_HEAT | rolled (the seed: each even, at least one [ASSUMED]) | the body engine (`has`): a body that does not eat builds no hunger, one that does not breathe does not drown |
| Body | mass | 70 kg, or W in the seed's code | default / rolled | the diet's minimum (mass to the 0.75, Kleiber) |
| Body | warmth | its own, or none (H): a cold body rests at 15 % of a warm one's energy (Bennett & Ruben 1979: endotherms five- to tenfold) | rolled | the diet's minimum; the touch tell; the heat model (section 107): no regulation, heat at 15 %, the body at the temperature around it, no hypothermia or heat stroke |
| Attributes | strength, agility, toughness, endurance, recuperation, disease resistance; analytical ability, focus, willpower, creativity, intuition, patience, memory, linguistic ability, spatial sense, musicality, kinesthetic sense, empathy, social awareness | each the median of one of the raws' human-like creatures (human 1000 throughout; dwarf, elf, goblin, minotaur as set in their ranges), each creature as likely [ASSUMED] | rolled | strength scales grip (`strength.ts`) in proportion [ASSUMED]; the rest stand in the sheet for the narrator |
| Senses | sight, hearing, smell | 1 × a human's each | default | sight: the vision model (human acuity) |
| Senses | sees in the dark | with the share of the raws' creatures that do (EXTRAVISION, 37 of 767) | rolled | the vision model (section 108): TrueNature gives the flag; Look, the combat sight check, footsteps seen and gestures made out take the light 6 times brighter (a cat's threshold about a sixth of a person's) |
| Speed | top speed | 100, a human's (Cataclysm's convention) | default | the sheet, for the narrator |
| Mind | speaks; fear | speaks (it passes for people); fear unless NOFEAR | default / computed | the sheet |
| Time | active | by day with the raws' share of DIURNAL creatures (142 of 767), by night with NOCTURNAL's (54), else at any hour (CREPUSCULAR and VESPERTINE counted so [ASSUMED]) | rolled | the world book's activity line; `days.ts`: one of a kind active by night keeps the night, their whole day twelve hours later (section 106) |
| Time | lifespan; healing | a human's (the engine's life tables; its wound healing) | default | the body engine |
| Numbers | how new ones come | by origin [ASSUMED rates until measured ones are found]: born so, their children at the place's birth rate (the year's: England and Wales 33.5 per 1,000 in 1841, 34.0 in 1881, 25.5 in 1920; the others' children are human); made, 1 % of their number a year, from the living; came back from death, 1 % of the place's dead (the returning dead of an outbreak model take such a rate as a parameter: Munz, Hudea, Imad & Smith? 2009, "When zombies attack!"); came from elsewhere, 1 % of their number a year; all live, die and leave as people do (`kindMadeRate`, `kindReturnRate`, `kindArriveRate`, range 0.1–10 %) | computed | the world count (`world-count.ts`, section 110): the kind's number year by year on the World tab |
| Defences | resists | each resistance the body engine reads, with its share among the raws' creatures (NO_DIZZINESS 32, NOSTUN 23, NO_FEVERS 18, NONAUSEA 15, PARALYZEIMMUNE 5, FIREIMMUNE 5 of 767), plus pain and fear from the body | rolled | the body engine (`has`: venom symptoms, being stunned) |
| Defences | weak to | nothing | default | the sheet |
| Feeding | diet | its diet and least amount (seed.ts: flesh 32,000 kcal an adult's muscle, Cole 2017 as reported; blood about 780 kcal a litre [ASSUMED estimate]); a human's food without E | computed | the seed's ratings and outcome |
| Feeding | attacks | a human's in the raws: punch, kick, scratch, bite | data | the strike model (a human body) |
| Tells | what shows | computed from the body (`tells.ts`): no mist of breath, skin at the air's temperature; any written as laws beyond the engine | computed | Look and Touch |

The narrator reads the sheet in the world book line ("Its sheet, as it is, not as known"); the player is shown it in the seed's popup as the seed's law, which their character may not know.
