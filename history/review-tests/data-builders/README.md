# Builders of src/rules/data/senses.ts (kept outside the repo, as AGENTS.md asks)

1. `python3 -I wn.py english-wordnet-2024.xml.gz wn.pkl`: Open English WordNet 2024 (github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet, CC BY 4.0): noun lemmas, senses, lexicographer files, hypernyms, instance senses.
2. `python3 -I vn.py verbnet3.4 vn.json`: VerbNet 3.4 (github.com/cu-clear/verbnet): for each verb and class, the direct object's role in each frame, its selectional restrictions, frames without an object, two-object frames (since section 43 each object entry says whether it is a two-object frame's first object).
3. `python3 -I build.py nouns.txt vn.json senses.ts`: nouns.txt is the `NOUNS` string of src/rules/data/wordnet.ts; `types_mod.py` (the kinds by anchor synsets and lexicographer files) must sit beside it; paths inside point at the scratch folder they were run from. Since section 43: an unrestricted object in a role of amount (Value, Extent, Duration, Asset) must be a quantity, money or a time; a two-object frame's first object is marked "^"; footwear counts as a garment (r), roads and paths as places (l).
4. `python3 -I wnv.py english-wordnet-2024.xml.gz wnv.pkl`: WordNet's verb and noun senses with their synsets, definitions, examples, members, hypernyms and derivations.
5. `python3 -I gloss.py verbnet3.4 wnv.pkl src/rules/router.ts gloss.json`: for each verb whose VerbNet classes route to different tools, each class's Lesk words from the WordNet senses its members name (`wn` keys); the JSON is written into src/rules/data/sense-gloss.ts.
6. `python3 -I cues.py wn.pkl nouns.txt src/rules/data/cue-words.ts oewn.xml.gz` (section 43): for each tool, the lexicon's nouns whose first common sense (no instance, no genus) lies under its anchor synsets (listed in the script with their definitions), noun.feeling for Appraise, people who keep watch, and the adjectives WordNet links by "similar" to cold and hot.
7. `python3 -I activity.py wnv.pkl vn.json src/rules/data/activity-verbs.ts` (section 43): the verbs of the activities the tools' descriptions name (WordNet synsets chosen by definition, with their troponyms; a verb listed when its first sense lies there), the emotion verbs (VerbNet admire-31.2 or marvel-31.3, first WordNet sense in verb.emotion), and the running verbs (first sense under moving very fast, moving hurriedly, or fleeing).
